## Confirmed facts (primary)

1. **Requirements list** (P1). The requirements are listed, not ordered:
   - copy of the resolution approving the **IGAFOM** (Instrumento de
     Gestión Ambiental para la Formalización Minera);
   - **authorization to use the surface land** (official format);
   - **proof of title to the mining concession**;
   - **sworn statement that there are no archaeological remains**
     (official format);
   - submission of the **Expediente Técnico**.

   A separate official format is used to request the **start or restart
   of mining activities**.
2. **Channel** (P1)(P2). The procedure is online, through MINEM's
   Ventanilla Única de Formalización Minera. The DGFM (MINEM) is the
   contact point.
3. **Legal status** (P2). Ley Nº 32537 amends D.L. 1293 and extends the
   *proceso de formalización minera integral* until **31 Dec 2026**, or
   until the Ley MAPE and its regulation enter into force, whichever
   comes first. MINEM is the governing body ("ente rector").
4. **New obligations under Ley 32537** (P2):
   - REINFO registrants must report the real georeferenced location of
     their operations within 120 calendar days, exclusively through the
     Ventanilla Única;
   - regional governments must transfer the process's entire document
     archive to MINEM within 60 days;
   - SUNAFIL will verify that REINFO registrants put their workers on
     payroll;
   - a national MAPE census will be run by INEI, MINEM and Ingemmet.

## Secondary / not independently verified

- Ley MAPE: MINEM expected to present it to Congress by 15 Oct 2026
  (Rumbo Minero, Infobae, Sep 2026). Not enacted as of this research.
- Cross-cutting requirements: RUC and IQBF registration (secondary only).

## Proposed website copy (4–6 steps, tú)

Only sourced facts. No deadlines in the copy, because they change by
law. The ordering is a presentation choice, not an official sequence
((P1) lists the requirements unordered).

1. **Revisamos tu situación en el REINFO** — Verificamos tu inscripción
   y lo que te falta para avanzar en la formalización.
2. **Acreditamos la concesión** — Reunimos la titularidad o el contrato
   que te permite trabajar la concesión minera.
3. **Aseguramos el terreno superficial** — Gestionamos la autorización de
   uso del terreno donde operas.
4. **Preparamos el IGAFOM** — Elaboramos tu instrumento de gestión
   ambiental y lo acompañamos hasta su aprobación.
5. **Armamos el expediente técnico** — Integramos el expediente y la
   declaración jurada de inexistencia de restos arqueológicos.
6. **Solicitamos el inicio de actividades** — Presentamos tu solicitud de
   inicio o reinicio por la Ventanilla Única del MINEM.
